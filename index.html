Vor der Prüfung

Exam Checklist

Check these before submitting any written task.

  • Every sentence has a verb in position 2
  • All nouns are capitalized (das Buch, die Mutter, der Vater)
  • I used the correct article: der (m) / die (f) / das (n)
  • Possessives match the noun gender (mein / meine)
  • Dativ after: mit, bei, nach, seit, von, zu, aus, gegenüber
  • Akkusativ after: durch, für, gegen, ohne, um
  • Separable verb prefix is at the END of the sentence
  • Perfekt: haben/sein in pos. 2 + Partizip II at the END
  • Modal verb in pos. 2 + infinitive at the END
  • Letter has: Greeting · Body (30 words) · Closing + Name
